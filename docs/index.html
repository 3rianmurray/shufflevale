Getting started

A short guided tour starts the first time you open the app. To see it again, open the menu (☰) on the home screen and choose Help, then Replay the Tutorial. Help also lists every gesture.

Common questions

Where are my decks stored? Only on your device, and they're included in its iCloud Backup. With Shufflevale Pro you can also keep them safe in Files and bring them to a new device: Back up everything in the home screen's menu, or back up a single deck, group or table from its own menu.

What does Shufflevale Pro add? The free version holds up to 200 of your own cards (the built-in Playing Cards deck doesn't count), with dice, counters, timers, score pads and every other table tool. Opening a Shufflevale file someone sends you is free too, and you can play with any custom art on it. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription:

  • Unlimited cards. No 200-card limit: every deck for every game, and every prototype.
  • Custom pieces. Add custom art to the table: game boards, player dashboards, score tracks, and more. Plus custom tokens and your own faces on dice and coins.
  • Share decks and tables. Send a deck, a group or a whole table to a friend as one file. Opening it is free for them.
  • Back up. Keep everything safe in Files, and bring it to a new device.
  • Support Shufflevale. Made by one person. Every Pro keeps it growing.

I bought Pro but it's locked. Open the menu (☰), choose Shufflevale Pro, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

I deleted the Solitaire table or the Playing Cards deck. Open the menu (☰) and choose Restore examples.

How do I bring in a sheet of many cards at once? In a deck, tap + Add and choose Import Sprite Sheet. Shufflevale works out the grid for you.
